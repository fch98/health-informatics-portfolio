## Applied Methodological Frameworks

* **Systematic Review & PRISMA Synthesis:** Applying PRISMA flow standards to map scientific identification, screening, eligibility, and inclusion across biomedical literature databases.
* **Predictive Model Appraisal:** Evaluating clinical machine learning applications against clinical endpoints, assessing false-positive vs. false-negative trade-offs in acute inpatient environments.
* **Health Information Exchange (HIE) Research:** Assessing quantitative and qualitative outcomes of data sharing between health networks on patient readmission frequencies and redundant diagnostic procedures.
* **Scientific Dissemination:** Translating clinical informatics findings into accessible executive summaries, visual conference posters, and peer-reviewed manuscript formats.
