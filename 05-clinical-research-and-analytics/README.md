Strategic health systems consulting deliverables, clinical workflow process optimization, and healthcare technology governance assessments. This module analyzes operational workflows, patient journey transitions, and ethical considerations surrounding automated clinical algorithms.

---

## Technical Competencies & Framework

| Focus Area | Core Methodologies & Frameworks | Applied Implementation Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clinical Workflow Modeling** | BPMN 2.0, Swimlane Diagrams | Mapping clinical care pathways to uncover operational delays, redundant tasks, and care coordination gaps. |
| **Systems Requirements Analysis** | Functional Specs, DFDs, Root-Cause Analysis | Evaluating clinical information architectures and designing targeted intervention strategies. |
| **Algorithmic Bias & AI Ethics** | Algorithmic Fairness, Health Equity Frameworks | Evaluating Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS) for racial, socioeconomic, and statistical bias. |
| **Enterprise Consulting** | Strategic Problem Statements, KPI Mapping | Structuring actionable executive recommendations for hospital networks and regional systems. |
