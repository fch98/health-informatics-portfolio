This module compiles applied artifacts, strategic analyses, operational workflows, and policy frameworks across health system enterprises. It documents competencies in diagnosing clinical workflow bottlenecks, evaluating health policy and legal standards, designing quality improvement interventions, and directing organizational change.

---

## Core Analytical Methodologies

* **Workflow Diagnosis:** Process modeling to eliminate redundant clinician documentation, reduce intake latency, and optimize clinical decision support utilization.
* **Systems Interventions:** Designing multi-disciplinary care coordination protocols that bridge acute inpatient encounters with longitudinal outpatient management.
* **Organizational Transition:** Structuring evidence-based change management roadmaps to support high-adoption electronic health record (EHR) and clinical software implementations.
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Evaluating data rights, patient privacy boundaries, and ethical implications surrounding applied artificial intelligence and secondary health data use.
